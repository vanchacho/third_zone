Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains what Third Zone, LLC ("Third Zone", "we") collects through third-zone.com and how we use it. In short: we collect only what you send us through the contact form, we use it to reply, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, company, email address, the option you choose ("I am a…") and your message.
  • Basic technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking on this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and follow up on the conversation you started.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Netlify hosts this site and receives contact form submissions.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts on this site, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your message for as long as we need it to respond and to keep a record of our business conversations. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email contact@third-zone.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to object to or restrict how we use your data and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
