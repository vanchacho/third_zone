Play hospitality

Hospitality standards, applied to play.

We don't measure the throw. We measure the person — and tune the game so they come back. Most players need to feel they're winning; one in five needs a real challenge. The game recognizes this and adapts to each one.

Adaptive difficulty

The principle video games have used for decades, applied to physical play. No money on outcomes — not gambling.

The hospitality standard

Every guest treated like a regular: private scores, a host who asks your advice, a free round the moment anything breaks.

20 years in code

These rules aren't in any book. 4,000+ events, read by eye, now written into software that learns from every session.
