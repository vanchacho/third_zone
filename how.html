The pipeline

Three layers, one instant score.

The same stack runs under every game — which is exactly why we can keep adding new ones without rebuilding a thing.

📡

1 · Capture

High-speed cameras and motion sensors record every move — clubhead speed, ball spin, punch force, launch angle, placement — at 240 frames a second.

🧠

2 · Understand

Computer-vision models read the raw capture frame by frame and reconstruct exactly what your body and the ball did — no tags, no wearables.

🏆

3 · Score & compete

The engine grades the shot in under a tenth of a second, normalises it across games, and ranks it live — head-to-head, leaderboard or team play.

In every bay

The hardware behind the play.

Each arena bay is a self-contained kit — the same components in every venue, calibrated to work together out of the box.

🖥️

Arena screen

A wall-sized, high-brightness display renders stadiums, moving targets and your live score all around you.

🎥

High-speed cameras

Multi-angle 240fps cameras catch the exact frame of contact, even on the fastest swings.

📡

Radar & motion sensors

Doppler radar and optical sensors clock speed, spin, force and angle the instant you play.

💻

On-site edge compute

A local GPU box scores everything in real time — no cloud round-trip, no lag on the board.

🔊

Sound & lighting

Spatial audio and neon cues turn a personal best into a crowd-roar moment the whole room feels.

🏌️

Smart equipment

Real clubs, bats, bows and balls — the gear you'd actually use, tracked to the millimetre.

Good to know

Common questions.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Every game is built for all levels, and clubs, bats, bows and coaching are on the house.

How accurate is it?

Reads land within about 96% of pro-grade launch monitors, and every bay is calibrated daily to stay there.

Do I have to wear sensors?

No wearables and no tags — cameras and sensors in the bay do all the tracking while you just play.

Does every game use the same tech?

Yes. One capture-and-scoring stack powers all nine games, so new ones plug straight in.

Can groups and teams play together?

Absolutely — solo, one-on-one, team relays and full tournaments all run on the same live board.
