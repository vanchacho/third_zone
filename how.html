The pipeline

Three layers, one instant score.

The same stack runs under every game — which is exactly why we can keep adding new ones without rebuilding a thing.

1 · Capture

High-speed cameras and motion sensors record every move — clubhead speed, ball spin, punch force, launch angle, placement — at 240 frames a second.

2 · Understand (Edge AI)

Proprietary computer-vision models run on a local GPU in the venue, reading the raw capture frame by frame to reconstruct exactly what your body and the ball did — no tags, no wearables, no cloud round-trip.

3 · Score & compete

The engine grades the shot in under a tenth of a second, normalises it across games, and ranks it live — head-to-head, leaderboard or team play.

System architecture

Hardware to scoreboard, end to end.

Capture and inference both run on-site at the edge — no cloud round-trip in the scoring path. That's what keeps the number on the wall under a tenth of a second behind your body.

Good to know

Common questions.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. Every game is built for all levels, and clubs, bats, bows and coaching are on the house.

How accurate is it?

Reads land within about 96% of pro-grade launch monitors, and every bay is calibrated daily to stay there.

Do I have to wear sensors?

No wearables and no tags — cameras and sensors in the bay do all the tracking while you just play.

Does every game use the same tech?

Yes. One capture-and-scoring stack powers all six games, so new ones plug straight in.

Can groups and teams play together?

Absolutely — solo, one-on-one, team relays and full tournaments all run on the same live board.
