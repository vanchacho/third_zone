How it works

We don't just score your shot. We learn what keeps you playing.

A target wall, simple sensors and an engine built on 20 years of watching people play.

A visit

Five steps at the wall.

1

Step up

Rules on screen in under 30 seconds.

2

Warm up

3

Throw

Aim for the lit holes.

4

Score

Points land on your own screen.

5

Come back

Your profile makes the next visit fit you.

The engine

Every player gets their own game.

People differ in what keeps them playing. Most need to feel they're winning. About one in five needs a real challenge and stays hooked when they lose more than they win. The game recognizes this and adapts to each player.

Adaptive difficulty

The same principle video games have used for decades, applied to physical play.

Not gambling

Nothing is wagered, and no cash prize is ever tied to an adjusted outcome.

Every venue makes it smarter

It starts from the founder's rules and learns from every session.

Where we are

Prototype in progress. Pilot next.

A 90-day pilot at 2–3 venues will measure who plays again the same night and who comes back.
