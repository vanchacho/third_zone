The pipeline

Three layers, one instant score.

One stack under every game — so new games plug straight in.

1 · Capture

Cameras and sensors record every move at 240 frames a second.

2 · Understand

Models reconstruct exactly what your body and the ball did — no tags, no wearables.

3 · Score & compete

The engine grades and ranks the shot live, in under a tenth of a second.

In every bay

The hardware behind the play.
