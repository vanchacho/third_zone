Platform · Roadmap

Where it's proven

Our own arenas.

We run our own rooms for three reasons: to prove the technology in the wild, to collect the data no one else has, and to show licensees exactly what the product does to a P&L.

Venues · Proof

A live showroom

Every prospective licensee can walk into a working arena and watch guests play — the shortest path from pitch to signature.

Venues · Footprint

Fits the empty floor

Our arenas drop into vacant urban space that big-box entertainment can't use. Fast and low-cost to fit out, so each room opens sooner.

Venues · Revenue

One guest pays twice

Guests pay to play — by session, round or membership — and again for food and drinks. Two streams from the same square foot, on the same visit.

Venues · Retention

The rematch engine

Live scores and leaderboards turn one visit into a standing plan. Competition, not novelty, is what brings guests back.
