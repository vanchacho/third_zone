How it works

Baseball, step by step.

1

Gear up

Grab a bat and a helmet — everything's provided. Just show up and step in.

2

Step into the bay

A netted arena bay with the city skyline behind you. Your name goes on the board.

3

Swing

Stand in and take your cuts. The machine feeds pitches while sensors track every contact.

4

Beat the board

The system turns every hit into a live score. The next player tries to top it.

What the system reads

Three numbers, live on the board.

Exit velocity

How fast the ball leaves the bat — pure power off contact.

Ways to play

Solo, one-on-one, or the whole crew.

Mode · Solo

Solo practice

Warm up and chase your own personal best, shot after shot, at your own pace.

Mode · 1v1

Head-to-head

Two players, one board. Highest score takes the round — first to three wins the match.

Mode · Team

Team relay

Split into teams and stack everyone's scores. The great equaliser for mixed groups.

Mode · Event

Tournament

Bracketed rounds with a live leaderboard and a winner crowned at the end of the night.
