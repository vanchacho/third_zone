The engine

Every player gets their own game.

People differ in what keeps them playing. Most need to feel they're winning. One in five needs a real challenge and stays hooked when they lose more than they win. The game recognizes this and adapts to each player.

Adaptive difficulty

The same principle video games have used for decades, applied to physical play.

Not gambling

Nothing is wagered, and no cash prize is ever tied to an adjusted outcome.

Gets smarter

It starts from the founder's rules and learns from every session. Every venue makes it smarter.
