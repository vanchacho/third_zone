Games, one platform

Special arena screen

A wall-sized, high-brightness display wraps each bay — stadiums, targets and your live score, rendered all around you.

Computer-vision AI

Trained models watch every swing, throw and shot frame by frame and understand exactly what you did — no wearables, no tags.

Motion & impact sensors

Radar and optical sensors capture speed, spin, force, launch angle and placement the instant you play.

Live scoring engine

Our software fuses every signal, normalises it across games and lands a number on the board in under a tenth of a second.

Self-learning models

Every round played feeds the system. The more you all play, the sharper the reads and the fairer the scoring get.

Player profiles & leaderboard

Personal bests, live standings and instant rematches — your scores follow you from one visit to the next.
